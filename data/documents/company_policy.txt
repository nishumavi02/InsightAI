InsightAI Sample Company Policy

Employees are entitled to 18 paid leave days per year.

Unused paid leave can be carried forward up to 10 days into the next calendar year.

Employees can work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

The standard working week is Monday to Friday.

Employees should submit leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.

For urgent leave, employees should inform their manager as soon as possible.

The company provides annual performance reviews to all full-time employees.
